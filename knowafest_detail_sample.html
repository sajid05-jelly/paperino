About Event

The Department of Information Technology, in association with the Industry Institute Partnership Cell and the Centre of Excellence in Data Science, Kongu Engineering College, proudly presents a National Level Workshop on Applied Generative AI: Foundations and Vibe Coding.

This one-day hands-on workshop is designed to introduce participants to the fundamentals of Generative AI, prompt engineering, and AI-assisted coding techniques. The sessions focus on practical implementation, enabling students to build real-world AI-powered applications while gaining industry-relevant skills.
